# SplitLedger Support

Welcome to the official support repository for **SplitLedger** — the financial management platform built for organizations, clubs, and teams.

This repository is used for:

- 🐛 **Bug reports** — Something not working as expected? Let us know.
- 💡 **Feature requests** — Have an idea that would improve SplitLedger? We'd love to hear it.
- 💬 **Discussions** — General questions, feedback, or community conversation.

> ⚠️ **This repository does not contain source code.** For security-related issues, please **do not** open a public issue — instead, email us at [security@splitleger.pro](mailto:security@splitleger.pro).

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

Before opening an issue, please:

1. **Search existing issues** to avoid duplicates — your question may already be answered.
2. **Check our [Discussions](../../discussions)** tab for broader conversations.
3. **Use the correct template** when creating a new issue (see below).

---

## 📋 Issue Types

| Type | When to use |
|---|---|
| 🐛 Bug Report | Something is broken or behaving unexpectedly |
| 💡 Feature Request | You'd like to suggest a new feature or improvement |
| ❓ Question / Support | You need help with something specific |

---

## 💬 Community Guidelines

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Provide as much detail as possible — vague reports are hard to act on.
- One issue per report; don't bundle multiple bugs into one ticket.
- Issues that don't follow the templates may be closed without response.

---

## 📬 Contact

For business inquiries or anything not suited for public issues, reach out at [support@splitleger.pro](mailto:support@splitleger.pro).
